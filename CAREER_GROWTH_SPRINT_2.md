## 1. Executive Overview

### Objective

Expand Locitra's `career-growth` category from **31 to 41 high-authority articles** by executing **Sprint 2**—a 10-article strategic expansion (~38,400 projected total words). Sprint 2 captures high-volume, high-RPM search queries across three key career evolution vectors:

1. **AI Job Search & Interview Conversion**: AI Mock Interviews, Prompt Engineering Certifications, Non-Technical AI Product Management transitions.
2. **Compensation & Global Career Arbitrage**: Tech Compensation/Equity (RSUs, Base, Bonus) Benchmarking, Remote Global Salary Arbitrage, AI Solopreneur Side-Hustles.
3. **Future-Proofing & Executive AI Leadership**: Mid-Career AI Upskilling, AI-Native Team Management, Personal Branding for Tech Leaders, Automation Career Resilience.

### Scope & Targets

- **Articles to Execute**: 10 new MDX articles (1 Primary Pillar, 8 Supporting Guides, 1 Master Pillar Bridge).
- **Target Word Count**: 3,500 – 4,200 words per article (Editorial Target Average: ~3,840 words).
- **Category Focus**: `career-growth` with deep cross-category mesh connections to `ai-tools`, `software-reviews`, `technology`, `online-income`, and `success-stories`.
