## 3. Key Gap Classifications & Attack Strategy

### 3.1 Missing & Underserved Topics (High-Opportunity Gaps)

1. **AI Interview Practice & Mock Tools (`ai-interview-preparation-guide-2026`)**: Competitors either offer paywalled academic opinion (HBR) or ad-bloated listicles (Forbes). No publication provides a clean, step-by-step prompt loop for practicing behavioral and technical mock interviews with ChatGPT paired with a neutral SaaS matrix.
2. **Global Remote Compensation Arbitrage (`global-compensation-arbitrage-remote-2026`)**: Legacy tech portals (BuiltIn, The Muse) remain strictly US-centric. Vendor sites (Deel, Rippling) have inherent commercial bias. Locitra can dominate this space by providing neutral legal, tax, and purchasing-power arbitrage blueprints.

### 3.2 Over-Served vs. High-Quality Differentiation Gaps

1. **AI Side Hustles (`ai-side-hustle-to-full-time-career-2026`)**: Over-served on YouTube and low-tier blogs with clickbait "get rich quick with AI" claims. Locitra's differentiation strategy focuses on **realistic financial risk management, legal structuring, and productized automation services**.
2. **Tech Compensation & Equity (`tech-compensation-equity-negotiation-2026`)**: Platforms like Levels.fyi provide data tables but lack long-form negotiation playbooks. Locitra leverages verifiable compensation data to provide tactical negotiation scripts for RSUs, options, and signing bonuses.
