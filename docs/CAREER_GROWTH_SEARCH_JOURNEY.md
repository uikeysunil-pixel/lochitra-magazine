## 1. Executive Summary

The Locitra **User Search Journey Model** defines how readers navigate through the 9 stages of modern career evolution—from initial job search friction to long-term executive resilience. By mapping each Sprint 1 and Sprint 2 article to explicit career transition milestones, Locitra creates an immersive, multi-touchpoint content ecosystem that maximizes session duration, newsletter conversions, and affiliate monetization.

## 3. Transition Support & Reader Intent Mapping

### Stage 1 → Stage 2: From Discovery to Application

- **User Intent**: The reader realizes their traditional job search tactics are failing or that ATS filters are rejecting their resume.
- **Locitra Transition Mechanism**: Articles in Stage 1 provide explicit in-text CTAs leading to `ats-resume-optimization-guide-2026` and `best-ai-resume-builders-for-job-seekers-2026`.

### Stage 2 → Stage 3: From Application to Interview Conversion

- **User Intent**: Having optimized their resume, the job seeker receives interview callbacks but experiences anxiety around AI-assisted screening or technical case studies.
- **Locitra Transition Mechanism**: `best-ai-resume-builders-for-job-seekers-2026` concludes with a dedicated "Next Step" callout linking directly to `ai-interview-preparation-guide-2026` (Sprint 2 A01).

### Stage 3 → Stage 4: From Interview to High-Value Offer Negotiation

- **User Intent**: The candidate passes final-round interviews and receives a complex job offer containing base salary, annual performance bonus, and equity (RSUs or stock options).
- **Locitra Transition Mechanism**: `ai-interview-preparation-guide-2026` links to `tech-compensation-equity-negotiation-2026` (Sprint 2 A03) to help candidates benchmark market rates and execute counter-offers.

### Stage 4 → Stage 5: From Local Employment to Global Arbitrage

- **User Intent**: The professional secures a US or EU remote role and seeks to maximize net income by moving to lower-cost jurisdictions while maintaining corporate compensation.
- **Locitra Transition Mechanism**: `tech-compensation-equity-negotiation-2026` bridges to `global-compensation-arbitrage-remote-2026` (Sprint 2 A06).

### Stage 6 → Stage 7: From Individual Contributor to AI Product Lead / Solopreneur

- **User Intent**: Mid-career knowledge workers seek higher leverage, evaluating whether to pivot into AI Product Management or launch an AI-driven side hustle.
- **Locitra Transition Mechanism**: `ai-upskilling-playbook-mid-career-2026` provides internal links to `ai-product-management-non-technical-2026` (Sprint 2 A05) and `ai-side-hustle-to-full-time-career-2026` (Sprint 2 A08).

### Stage 8 → Stage 9: From Manager to Master Strategic Leader

- **User Intent**: Experienced managers and directors focus on executive presence, team automation governance, and long-term career resilience against AI disruption.
- **Locitra Transition Mechanism**: Leadership articles funnel directly into the master pillar `future-proof-career-automation-era-2026` (Sprint 2 A10) and category hub `career-growth-2026`.
