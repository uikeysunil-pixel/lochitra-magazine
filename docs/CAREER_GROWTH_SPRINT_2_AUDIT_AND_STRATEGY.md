### 3.2 SERP Feature Landscape & AI Overview Dynamics

Analysis of Google SERP features across Sprint 2 target queries highlights specific layout requirements:

1. **Google AI Overviews (SGE)**: Estimated prevalence on ~68% of informational queries (e.g., `ai upskilling roadmap`, `future proof your career ai`). _Strategy_: Locitra articles must incorporate formatted bulleted summaries and clear sub-heading answers immediately following H2 tags to maximize AI Overview citation capture.
2. **People Also Ask (PAA)**: Present on 100% of analyzed target SERPs. _Strategy_: Every Sprint 2 article includes a dedicated H2 `## Frequently Asked Questions` formatted with 4–5 H3 question headings addressing high-volume PAA queries.
3. **Reddit & Discussion Forum Modules**: High SERP prominence on query terms like `how to practice interviews with ai` and `tech salary negotiation rsu`. _Strategy_: Incorporate real-world practitioner insights, community consensus quotes, and unsanctioned "in-the-trenches" case studies to match Reddit's authenticity signals.
