## 1. Executive Summary

Under the **Locitra Editorial Operating System (LEOS v2.0)**, publication is not the end of the content lifecycle—it is the beginning of continuous operational optimization. Post-publication performance tracking ensures that every published article satisfies its traffic, authority, and revenue projections while maintaining technical integrity and E-E-A-T trust.

This document establishes the official **Post-Publication Success Metrics Framework** for Career Growth Sprint 2. It defines the review cadence, key performance indicators (KPIs), ownership responsibilities, success thresholds, and reporting protocols at **30-Day**, **90-Day**, and **180-Day** post-deployment milestones.

## 3. Milestone Review Specifications

### 3.1 30-Day Review Specification (Technical & Indexation Gate)

The 30-Day Review evaluates whether all 10 Sprint 2 articles have been correctly indexed by Google and validated for technical health.

#### Key Metrics Tracked:

1. **Pages Published**: Confirm 10/10 MDX files in production (`draft: false`).
2. **Indexing Status**: Google Search Console (GSC) index verification (Target: 100% indexed).
3. **Search Console Impressions**: Initial query impression volume (Target: >10,000 combined 30-day impressions).
4. **Average Position**: Baseline rank tracking for primary target keywords.
5. **Click-Through Rate (CTR)**: Early CTR performance on impressions.
6. **Crawl Status**: Verify zero 4xx/5xx errors or mobile usability flags in GSC.
7. **Internal Links Completed**: Confirm 210/210 planned internal link edges exist and resolve.
8. **Technical Validation**: Next.js build clean compliance; 0 hydration errors.
9. **Schema Validation**: Google Rich Results Test validation for `Article`, `FAQPage`, `HowTo`, and `ItemList`.

---

### 3.2 90-Day Review Specification (Traffic Traction & Engagement Gate)

The 90-Day Review assesses organic search traction, keyword ranking growth, and reader engagement across all Sprint 2 sub-clusters.

#### Key Metrics Tracked:

1. **Organic Clicks**: Monthly organic clicks generated by Sprint 2 content (Target: >8,400 monthly clicks).
2. **Top 20 Rankings**: Number of target keywords ranking in positions 1–20.
3. **Top 10 Rankings**: Number of target keywords ranking on Page 1 (positions 1–10).
4. **Featured Snippets**: Number of paragraph, list, or table snippets captured.
5. **AI Overview Visibility**: Citation tracking in Google AI Overviews.
6. **Newsletter Signups**: Subscriptions generated via `<BlogNewsletterForm />` callouts.
7. **Affiliate Clicks**: Outbound click volume to SaaS & EdTech affiliate partners.
8. **Average Engagement Time**: Reader retention and dwell time (Target: >3m 30s average).
9. **Returning Visitors**: Reader return rate driven by internal mesh links.

---

### 3.3 180-Day Review Specification (Monetization ROI & Authority Gate)

The 180-Day Review evaluates full commercial performance, category traffic expansion, and informs the Phase 0 strategy for Sprint 3.

#### Key Metrics Tracked:

1. **Revenue Contribution**: Combined AdSense and affiliate revenue generated by Sprint 2 articles.
2. **Category Traffic Growth**: Total percentage growth of `career-growth` category traffic (Baseline: ~72k words -> Target: ~111k words).
3. **Topical Authority Expansion**: Evaluation of keyword cluster dominance across search engines.
4. **Cannibalization Review**: Audit for any internal ranking friction between Sprint 1 and Sprint 2 articles.
5. **Internal Link Audit**: Review and optimization of anchor text distributions and link equity flow.
6. **Refresh Candidates**: Identification of underperforming articles requiring editorial updates.
7. **New Keyword Opportunities**: Extraction of long-tail secondary queries revealed in GSC performance logs.
8. **Sprint 3 Recommendations**: Formulation of data-backed recommendations for the next expansion sprint.

## 4. Key Performance Indicator (KPI) Definitions

| KPI Name                      | Definition                                                       | Measurement Method           | Target Baseline                     |
| :---------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------- | :---------------------------------- |
| **Indexation Rate**           | Percentage of published sprint pages indexed in Google.          | Google Search Console API    | 100% (10/10 pages)                  |
| **Page 1 Rank Rate**          | Percentage of primary keywords ranking in Top 10.                | Rank Tracker / GSC           | >50% (5/10 keywords at 90 days)     |
| **Average RPM**               | AdSense revenue generated per 1,000 pageviews.                   | Google AdSense Analytics     | $50.00 – $85.00 RPM Range           |
| **Affiliate Conversion Rate** | Outbound affiliate clicks divided by unique article visits.      | GA4 Event Tracking           | >3.5% on commercial intent articles |
| **Zero-Orphan Compliance**    | Percentage of sprint articles with bidirectional internal links. | LEOS Repository Audit Script | 100% (0 orphan pages)               |
