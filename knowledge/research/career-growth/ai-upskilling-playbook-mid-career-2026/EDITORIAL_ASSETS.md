## 1.2 Granular Tier Breakdown & Performance Criteria

### Tier 1: Foundational AI Literacy

- **Core Competencies**: Model architecture awareness (LLMs vs Diffusion models), hallucination detection, data privacy guidelines, zero-shot and few-shot prompting.
- **Actionable Milestone**: Successfully audit a 5-page business report using Claude 3.5 Sonnet while verifying all factual claims against primary sources.
- **Verification Criterion**: 100% compliance with corporate data safety rules (zero PII or confidential IP entered into public models).

### Tier 2: Applied Tool Mastery

- **Core Competencies**: System prompt architecture, persona adoption, Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning loops, multi-modal analysis (vision + document analysis).
- **Actionable Milestone**: Build a personal library of 15 role-specific master system prompts that cut weekly drafting time in half.
- **Verification Criterion**: Consistently generate publication-ready business deliverables with fewer than 2 manual edit passes.

### Tier 3: No-Code Automation & Workflows

- **Core Competencies**: Webhook configuration, JSON payload handling, Custom GPT Actions, multi-agent chaining in Make.com/Zapier.
- **Actionable Milestone**: Deploy an automated workflow that transcribes team meeting recordings, extracts action items, updates Notion/Asana, and drafts follow-up emails automatically.
- **Verification Criterion**: Zero workflow crashes over 30 consecutive execution cycles.

### Tier 4: Strategic AI Leadership & Governance

- **Core Competencies**: Corporate AI policy drafting, vendor security evaluation, team upskilling roadmap design, ROI tracking metrics.
- **Actionable Milestone**: Present a board-ready 1-year AI Transformation Strategy for your department, including a projected 3x ROI on software spend.
- **Verification Criterion**: Successful adoption of AI tools by at least 75% of managed team members within 60 days.

# SECTION 2 — MID-CAREER AI READINESS SCORECARD

## 2.1 20-Question Diagnostic Assessment

Evaluate your current workplace AI readiness by scoring each question on a scale of **0 to 5**:

- `0` = Completely Disagree / Never
- `1` = Rarely / Minimal Knowledge
- `2` = Sometimes / Basic Awareness
- `3` = Moderately / Consistent Use
- `4` = Frequently / Advanced Knowledge
- `5` = Mastered / Expert Execution

### Category A: AI Awareness & Ecosystem Knowledge

1. **Model Literacy**: I can clearly explain the operational differences between ChatGPT (OpenAI), Claude (Anthropic), and Perplexity Pro.
2. **Data Security**: I know exactly which types of company data can and cannot be safely entered into AI tools.
3. **Capability Boundaries**: I understand the key limitations of LLMs, including hallucinations, knowledge cutoff dates, and math errors.

### Category B: Advanced Prompt Writing

4. **Persona Definition**: I routinely assign specific professional roles, tone constraints, and target audiences to AI models before asking for output.
5. **Contextual Fuel**: I provide background documents, guidelines, and structural examples (few-shot prompting) rather than writing 1-line queries.
6. **Iterative Refinement**: I use multi-turn conversational loops (Chain-of-Thought) to refine AI outputs step-by-step rather than accepting initial responses.

### Category C: Workflow & Task Automation

7. **Daily Copilot Habits**: I use AI tools for at least 30 minutes every workday to assist with writing, analysis, or research.
8. **No-Code Tools**: I have experimented with or built automated workflows using platforms like Make.com, Zapier, or Custom GPTs.
9. **Administrative Delegation**: I routinely automate routine tasks like meeting summaries, email drafting, or document formatting using AI.

### Category D: Data Analysis & Document Intelligence

10. **Data Wrangling**: I know how to upload CSVs, spreadsheets, or PDFs into AI models to extract key trends, charts, or summaries.
11. **Research Speed**: I use AI research engines (Perplexity/NotebookLM) to synthesize long industry reports in under 15 minutes.
12. **Verification Protocol**: I systematically double-check all statistical claims and quotes generated by AI against trusted primary sources.

### Category E: Leadership, Strategy & Governance

13. **Team AI Guidance**: I have shared prompt templates or AI best practices with colleagues or direct reports to improve team output.
14. **Strategic Vision**: I can identify the top 3 high-leverage areas in my department that could be transformed by AI tools.
15. **Ethical Evaluation**: I actively evaluate AI tools for potential bias, fairness, customer impact, and compliance risks.

### Category F: Continuous Learning & Career Habits

16. **Skill Investment**: I dedicate at least 2 hours per week to testing new AI features, tools, or online tutorials.
17. **Tool Experimentation**: I have tested at least 5 different AI tools in the past 6 months outside of ChatGPT.
18. **Employer Support**: I have explored or requested company learning budgets or software subscriptions for AI upskilling.
19. **LinkedIn & Personal Brand**: My resume and LinkedIn profile reflect modern, applied AI skills using quantitative business metrics.
20. **Adaptability Mindset**: I view AI as an empowering copilot that enhances my 15+ years of domain experience rather than a threat to my job.

## 3.1 Phase 1: Days 1–30 — Foundation & Copilot Habit Integration

### Time Commitment: 15 Minutes / Day (1.75 Hours / Week)

- **Weekly Objectives**:
  - _Week 1_: Set up accounts (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity); complete data privacy audit; master basic prompt syntax.
  - _Week 2_: Transition 100% of initial document drafting and brainstorming to Claude 3.5 Sonnet.
  - _Week 3_: Build a personal text expander / prompt vault containing 10 master system prompts for daily work.
  - _Week 4_: Master Perplexity Pro and Google NotebookLM for 10x faster market research and document synthesis.
- **Daily Habits**:
  - Spend the first 15 minutes of every workday opening your AI copilot before checking email.
  - Prompt AI to summarize long email threads or draft initial meeting agendas.
- **Deliverables**: Personal 10-Prompt Vault; Google NotebookLM knowledge base of your department's core SOPs.
- **Recommended Tools**: ChatGPT Free/Plus, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Google NotebookLM, Perplexity Free.
- **Phase 1 Milestone**: Complete a complex 5-page project brief in under 45 minutes (a 60% time reduction).
- **Success Criteria**: 10 consecutive workdays with documented AI copilot usage.
- **Expected Outcome**: Total elimination of "blank page anxiety"; 5 hours saved per week on basic drafting.

---

## 3.2 Phase 2: Days 31–60 — Advanced Prompting & No-Code Automation

### Time Commitment: 30 Minutes / Day (3.5 Hours / Week)

- **Weekly Objectives**:
  - _Week 5_: Master advanced Chain-of-Thought (CoT) and Few-Shot prompting techniques for complex reasoning tasks.
  - _Week 6_: Build your first Custom GPT (or Claude Project) configured with your company's brand voice and SOP guidelines.
  - _Week 7_: Set up Fathom AI or Fireflies.ai to capture, transcribe, and analyze 100% of your business meetings.
  - _Week 8_: Build a multi-step no-code automation scenario in Make.com (e.g. Form Submission -> LLM Summary -> Slack/Email Notification).
- **Daily Habits**:
  - Use voice mode or meeting assistants during calls to capture action items automatically.
  - Run all data analysis tasks through ChatGPT Data Analyst or Rows.com before opening manual spreadsheets.
- **Deliverables**: 1 Functional Custom GPT for your department; 1 Active Make.com automated workflow.
- **Recommended Tools**: ChatGPT Team / Claude Pro, Make.com, Fathom AI, Notion AI, Rows.com.
- **Phase 2 Milestone**: Deploy an automated meeting-to-task pipeline that saves 3 hours of manual follow-up every week.
- **Success Criteria**: Zero manual meeting notes taken for 14 consecutive days; 1 published team Custom GPT.
- **Expected Outcome**: Shift from manual execution to automated task orchestration; 10 hours saved per week.

---

## 3.3 Phase 3: Days 61–90 — Portfolio Building & Strategic Workplace Positioning

### Time Commitment: 45 Minutes / Day (5 Hours / Week)

- **Weekly Objectives**:
  - _Week 9_: Document 3 concrete AI workflow proof-of-concepts built during Phase 1 & 2 (with quantitative before/after metrics).
  - _Week 10_: Complete a recognized online credential (e.g. Vanderbilt Prompt Engineering or Google AI Essentials).
  - _Week 11_: Update your resume, LinkedIn profile, and internal company bio with quantitative AI business metrics.
  - _Week 12_: Pitch an employer-funded AI learning budget or present an internal AI transformation lunch-and-learn to leadership.
- **Daily Habits**:
  - Track weekly hours saved and business KPI improvements resulting from your AI tool stack.
  - Share 1 AI workflow tip or prompt pattern weekly on LinkedIn or internal company Slack channels.
- **Deliverables**: 3-Project Non-Technical AI Portfolio (PDF/Web page); Updated LinkedIn Profile; Employer Budget Request Letter.
- **Recommended Tools**: Canva / Gamma.app (for portfolio presentation), LinkedIn, TopResume, Coursera.
- **Phase 3 Milestone**: Formal presentation of your AI workflow improvements to senior management or client stakeholders.
- **Success Criteria**: Verified completion of 1 professional AI certificate; successful delivery of 1 internal AI demo.
- **Expected Outcome**: Positioned as a recognized AI leader in your organization; eligible for promotion or 20%+ salary increase.
