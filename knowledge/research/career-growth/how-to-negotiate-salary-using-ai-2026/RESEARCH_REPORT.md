## 5. Verified Compensation Benchmarking Standards

### Calculating Target Compensation Bands

To prevent AI hallucination during compensation benchmarking, candidates must supply validated inputs:

1. **Base Salary Target**: Calculated using local/remote geographic tiering (Tier 1: SF/NYC/Seattle; Tier 2: Austin/Chicago/Denver; Tier 3: Regional/National Remote).
2. **Equity Valuation**:
   - _Public Companies_: RSU Value = `(Granted Shares × Current Stock Price) / Vesting Years`.
   - _Late-Stage Private (Series C+)_: Discounted RSU Value = `Shares × Estimated Preferred Price × 0.50 (Liquidity/Risk Factor)`.
   - _Early-Stage Startup (Seed/Series A)_: Options Value = Treat as $0 base, evaluate percentage ownership (`Shares / Fully Diluted Share Count`).
3. **The 10–20% Rule**: Initial counter-offers should generally target 10% to 20% above the initial offer midpoint, bounded by verified 75th percentile market data.

## 8. Verified AI Strengths vs. Human-Only Domain Boundaries

### Strengths of AI in Salary Negotiation

- **Neutralizes Emotional Anxiety**: AI provides objective, data-backed scripts, eliminating imposter syndrome and fear of confrontation.
- **Speed & Iteration**: Generates multiple counter-offer variations (soft touch, firm counter, competing offer leverage) in seconds.
- **Roleplay Preparation**: Allows candidates to simulate tough HR objections (e.g., "This is top of band," "We don't negotiate equity") repeatedly before live calls.

### Limitations & Human-Only Domain Boundaries

- **Real-Time Human Intuition**: AI cannot read micro-expressions, vocal hesitation, or subtle rapport signals during a live phone or in-person meeting.
- **Over-Reliance on Templates**: Generic AI prompts generate overly formal, cold, or robotic emails that damage rapport.
- **Hallucination Risk on Niche Compensation**: AI may hallucinate equity vesting norms for niche startups unless explicitly fed current market benchmarks.
