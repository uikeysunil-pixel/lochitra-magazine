## 5. COMPONENT BREAKDOWN & COMPREHENSIVE DEFINITIONS

### 5.1 The 11 Components of Total Compensation (TC)

1. **Base Salary**: Fixed annual cash earnings. Subject to internal salary band caps.
2. **Annual Performance Bonus**: Target percentage of base salary tied to personal and company OKRs.
3. **Signing Bonus (Sign-On)**: One-time upfront cash lump sum used to offset unvested equity or close base pay gaps without breaking internal parity.
4. **Restricted Stock Units (RSUs)**: Direct grant of company stock units that vest over time (typically 4-year schedule with 1-year cliff). Taxed as ordinary income upon vesting.
5. **Incentive Stock Options (ISOs)**: Right to buy private startup shares at a fixed strike price. Eligible for favorable capital gains tax treatment if holding periods are met.
6. **Non-Qualified Stock Options (NSOs)**: Stock options taxed upon exercise on the spread between strike price and fair market value (FMV).
7. **Employee Stock Purchase Plan (ESPP)**: Statutory program allowing employees to purchase company stock at up to a 15% discount using payroll deductions.
8. **Equity Refresh Grants**: Annual or bi-annual additional equity grants awarded to high performers to maintain total comp target levels as original grants vest.
9. **Retirement & Match**: 401(k) / pension contributions with employer match percentage.
10. **Health & Welfare Benefits**: Premium coverage for medical, dental, vision, life, and disability.
11. **Perks & Stipends**: Remote work budget, professional development stipend, wellness allowance, childcare credits.
