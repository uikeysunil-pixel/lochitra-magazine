# RESEARCH REPORT: Proton Pass

## 1. Executive Summary

Proton Pass is a privacy-focused password manager built by the Swiss company Proton. It differentiates itself through end-to-end encryption, open-source architecture, and integration with the wider Proton ecosystem (Proton Mail, VPN, Drive, Calendar). It offers a robust free tier and competitive paid plans, emphasizing secure credential sharing and integrated email aliases.

## 2. Current Product Status

Active and receiving frequent updates. Available across major platforms.

## 3. Official Sources

- Official Documentation: proton.me/pass
- Official Security Whitepaper: proton.me/pass/security
- Official Release Notes: proton.me/blog

## 4. Verified Features

- End-to-End Encryption (E2EE) for passwords, notes, and metadata
- Open-source and independently audited clients
- Integrated Hide-my-email aliases (unlimited on paid plans)
- Built-in 2FA authenticator (TOTP)
- Secure credential sharing (Proton Pass Vaults)
- Passkey support
- Proton Sentinel (advanced account protection on premium plans)

## 5. Verified Pricing

- **Proton Pass Free**: $0/mo. Includes unlimited logins, unlimited devices, and up to 10 hide-my-email aliases.
- **Proton Pass Plus**: ~$1.99/mo (billed annually). Adds unlimited hide-my-email aliases, integrated 2FA, and Proton Sentinel.
- **Proton Unlimited**: ~$9.99/mo. Bundles Proton Pass with premium versions of Proton Mail, Calendar, Drive, and VPN.

## 6. Platform Support

- Desktop: Windows, macOS, Linux (via desktop apps and browser extensions)
- Mobile: iOS, Android
- Browsers: Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Brave, Safari

## 7. Security Architecture

- End-to-end encryption using bcrypt and 256-bit AES-GCM
- Zero-knowledge encryption architecture (Proton cannot read vault data)
- Open-source clients with independent security audits (Cure53)
- Swiss privacy laws jurisdiction

## 8. Privacy Model

Strict no-logs policy, zero-knowledge architecture. No tracking or telemetry used for advertising.

## 9. Passkey Support

Fully supported across all major platforms for creating, storing, and using passkeys.

## 10. Recent Updates

- Introduction of advanced Passkey support
- Launch of dedicated desktop applications for Windows and macOS
- Proton Sentinel AI security enhancements

## 11. Strengths

- Exceptional privacy and security reputation
- Generous free plan (unlimited devices and items)
- Integrated email aliases are highly convenient
- Open-source and audited

## 12. Weaknesses

- Newer to the market compared to Bitwarden or 1Password
- UI/UX still maturing, some advanced organizational features lacking
- Ecosystem lock-in (best experienced with other Proton services)

## 13. Competitor Snapshot

- **vs. Bitwarden**: Both are open-source and offer great free plans. Bitwarden is more established with more granular enterprise controls, while Proton Pass has better email alias integration.
- **vs. 1Password**: 1Password offers a more polished UI and deeper ecosystem integrations (like SSH keys), but lacks a free tier.

## 14. Reader Persona

- **Ideal Users**: Privacy enthusiasts, current Proton ecosystem users, users wanting a strong free plan with email aliases.
- **Not Recommended For**: Users requiring deep enterprise-level policy management or those deeply entrenched in the Apple ecosystem (who might prefer iCloud Keychain).
