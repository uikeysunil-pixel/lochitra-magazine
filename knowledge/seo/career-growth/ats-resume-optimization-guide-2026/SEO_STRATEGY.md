## 3. SERP Positioning & Competitive Differentiation

### Flaws in Competitor SERP Content

1. **Promoting Dangerous "ATS Hacks"**: Legacy articles recommend white-text keyword stuffing or pasting full job descriptions at the bottom of resumes—tactics that modern NLP parsers flag as spam.
2. **Tool-Listicle Confusion**: Competitors confuse ATS optimization guides with software tool round-ups, offering superficial app lists instead of technical parsing rules.
3. **Ignoring Platform Differences**: Competitors treat all ATS software identically, ignoring critical differences between enterprise legacy parsers (Workday/Taleo) and modern tech parsers (Greenhouse/Lever/Ashby).
4. **Neglecting Human Recruiter Review**: Competitors optimize purely for algorithms, creating stiff, unreadable resumes that fail the secondary 6-second human recruiter screen.

### Locitra’s Competitive Moat (How We Win Rank #1)

- **Proprietary Locitra PASS Framework**: Present **PASS (Parsing, Alignment, Structure, Scoring)** as the definitive technical optimization methodology for 2026 job seekers.
- **Platform-Specific Parsing Mechanics**: Deconstruct exact parsing behaviors across Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, Taleo, and Ashby.
- **4 Copy-Paste RTCC Optimization Blueprints**: Provide production-ready prompts for hard-skill extraction, STAR+V bullet rewriting, and pre-submission ATS match scoring.
- **Evidence-Based Myth Busting Table**: Debunk dangerous white-text myths using verified data from SHRM and ATS vendor technical documentation.
- **Human-in-the-Loop Integrity**: Enforce mandatory human review to ensure resumes pass both automated ATS filters and secondary human recruiter screens.
