## 1. Search Intent Analysis & Journey Mapping

### Primary Search Intent: High-Income C-Suite Portfolio Career Architecture

- **User State**: The user is a senior director, VP, ex-founder, or C-suite executive (CTO, CMO, CFO, COO) experiencing corporate burnout, corporate downsizing, or seeking career autonomy. They want to transition away from single-employer W-2 employment into a high-income portfolio career managing 2–4 client retainers ($5k–$15k/mo per client) simultaneously.
- **Why It Matters**: The user does NOT want a generic freelancing guide (`/blog/freelancing-for-beginners-2026`) or general B2B consulting agency tutorial (`/blog/corporate-to-consulting-transition-guide-2026`). They require a specialized operational framework for positioning, contract structuring, time-block capacity allocation, and embedded executive governance.

### Secondary Search Intent: Tactical Pricing & Client Acquisition

- **User State**: Searching specifically for `how to become a fractional CTO/CMO/CFO`, `fractional executive retainer pricing guide`, `fractional leadership contract template`, or `fractional executive vs management consultant`.
- **Why It Matters**: Delivering **THE LOCITRA APEX FRAMEWORK (Authority Positioning, Portfolio Capacity, Executive Retainer Architecture, Execution of Embedded Leadership)** along with copy-paste AI prompt blueprints for board decks and advisory briefs satisfies this high-intent query segment.
