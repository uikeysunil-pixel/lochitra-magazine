## 1. Search Intent Analysis

### Primary Search Intent: Informational & Tactical Execution

- **User State**: The candidate either has a job offer in hand (48–72 hour countdown) or is approaching an annual performance review within 30 days. They are searching for a step-by-step, actionable framework to calculate their market rate and request more compensation without alienating the hiring manager or employer.
- **Why It Matters**: High urgency and high cognitive strain. The user does not want abstract theory; they require exact formulas, verified benchmarking methods, and copy-paste prompt templates.

### Secondary Search Intent: Transactional / Template Seeking

- **User State**: Searching specifically for `salary negotiation scripts with AI`, `counter offer email template 2026`, or `how to use ChatGPT for salary negotiation`.
- **Why It Matters**: Delivers immediate utility. Providing structured RTCC prompt blueprints and humanized email response templates directly satisfies this transactional query segment.

### Hidden User Intent: Anxiety Reduction & Confidence Building

- **User State**: Candidates dread salary negotiation due to fear of offer rescission, perceived greediness, or conflict aversion.
- **Why It Matters**: The article must address the psychological barrier early, proving through empirical data (e.g., GMU research showing 85%+ of employers expect negotiation) that negotiating is standard professional etiquette.

### Commercial Intent: HIGH (Targeting High-Earning Knowledge Workers)

- **User State**: Active mid-career professionals (engineers, managers, analysts) preparing for significant financial transactions ($10k–$50k+ salary increases).
- **Why It Matters**: This demographic possesses high purchasing power and high propensity to subscribe to productivity AI tools (ChatGPT Plus, Claude Pro, Perplexity Pro) and career advancement resources.

### Long-Term Evergreen Intent

- **User State**: Salary negotiation is a permanent workplace event.
- **Why It Matters**: Combining 2026 Pay Transparency laws with timeless negotiation principles (BATNA, value anchoring) ensures multi-year organic traffic retention.
